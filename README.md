# Shawn McNurney
I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Oregon State University with a minor in Computer Science, and I currently work as a Facilities Engineer specializing in controls.

My background in facilities engineering and controls naturally drew me to data science. Working daily with complex building management systems and continuous sensor arrays, I saw the potential to apply data science techniques—such as anomaly detection, signal processing, and predictive maintenance—to optimize large-scale mechanical systems. By studying data science, I hope to bridge the gap between physical infrastructure hardware and advanced operational diagnostics to drive energy efficiency and system reliability.
